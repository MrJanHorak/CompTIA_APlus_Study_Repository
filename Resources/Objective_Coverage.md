# Objective Coverage and Practice Map

Reviewed October 18, 2026 against CompTIA's [Core 1 objectives](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/34be017cb73211ef8985a6f347fbf652) and [Core 2 objectives](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/cefedfb2b8a511ef809306d06d323538), document version 4.0. Topic labels below are brief repository summaries; read the official documents for all details.

## How to Use This Map

- **Intro:** Relevant introductory notes exist. This is not a claim of exhaustive coverage, technical mastery or a tested lab.
- **Partial:** Notes exist, but material needs expansion; the final column identifies a concrete next step.
- Mark objectives mastered only after explaining the concepts and completing an appropriate unfamiliar task.
- Repository filenames organize domains; their numbers are not official objective IDs. For example, `Core_1/1.2_Networking.md` covers official domain 2.0.
- Compare all bullets in the official documents with your own notes and chosen course. This map does not replace those documents.

## Supporting Professional Practice

The [six-step troubleshooting framework](../Cheat_Sheets/Troubleshooting_Methodology.md) is useful on the job. CompTIA explicitly excludes the methodology itself from the V15 exam while retaining practical troubleshooting skills.
